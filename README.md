# CarbonChain

CarbonChain is a decentralized social impact platform that transforms carbon credit trading through community-verified impact measurement and binary outcome prediction markets. The platform combines local community validation with AI-powered satellite data verification to create trust scores that directly influence carbon credit pricing. This innovative approach enables fractional carbon credits and micro-investments, making environmental impact accessible to individuals in developing countries.

The platform leverages IoT sensors deployed across reforestation and renewable energy projects, integrated with prediction markets where community members stake tokens on environmental target outcomes. Smart contracts automatically execute payments based on verifiable data including tree survival rates from drone imagery, solar panel output tracking, and methane reduction measurements through air quality sensors. A multi-signature oracle system ensures consensus between IoT sensor data, satellite analysis, and community reports before minting carbon credits or settling trading outcomes.

CarbonChain creates income opportunities for local environmental guardians while incentivizing long-term project maintenance through an innovative staking mechanism that rewards accuracy and project longevity. The platform demonstrates real-world impact through partnerships with indigenous forest conservation projects, urban solar initiatives, and agricultural cooperatives adopting regenerative practices. A comprehensive social impact dashboard provides real-time transparency of environmental improvements linked to specific locations, empowering communities to directly benefit from environmental stewardship.
